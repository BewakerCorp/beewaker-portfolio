How to order

  1. Contact me on Discord and send your character references, pose and expression, outfit and important design details, background or atmosphere, intended use, and preferred deadline.
  2. I review all materials before accepting a request and confirming its final price. The order is confirmed only after I explicitly accept it.
  3. Please do not send payment before your order is accepted.
  4. I may decline any request that I am not comfortable with.

Payment & process

Payment is made through Boosty.

Orders up to $25 require full payment before work begins.

Orders above $25 require a 50% payment before work begins. The remaining 50% is paid after the protected final preview is approved. Clean full-resolution files are delivered only after full payment.

The initial sketch includes up to two rounds of revisions. After the sketch is approved, one minor correction is included. Mistakes on my side are corrected free of charge.

If the client cancels after work has begun, payments are non-refundable. The client may keep the latest completed stage they have received. An unpaid finished artwork is shown only as a compressed, watermarked preview.

Pricing & delivery

Additional character: +80% of the base price.

Highly complex character design: up to +50%. The exact amount depends on the references and is confirmed before payment.

Rush orders may be available, but their price and deadline are discussed individually.

The usual turnaround is up to two weeks after payment and receipt of all required materials. Time spent waiting for feedback, references, or approval is not included.

If the agreed deadline is missed due to a delay on my side, the remaining payment may be reduced depending on the length of the delay.

Final artwork is delivered through Discord as PNG and/or JPG. Source and project files are not included.

Usage

The finished artwork may be used for personal purposes, including as an avatar, and may be reposted with visible credit. My signature must not be removed.

Commercial use, resale, and redistribution for profit are not permitted unless agreed upon separately.

I may display the finished artwork in my portfolio and social media. Privacy can be discussed before the order is accepted.
