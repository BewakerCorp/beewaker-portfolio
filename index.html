Order & delivery

  1. Contact me on Discord and include the word MOTH in your first message.
  2. Please do not send payment before I accept your request.
  3. Payment through Boosty is requested only after the sketch is ready.
  4. The usual turnaround is up to two weeks after payment. The exact deadline is discussed individually.
  5. The finished artwork will be delivered through the same Discord conversation.
  6. I may decline any request that I am not comfortable with.

Usage

Personal use is allowed, including use as an avatar. When reposting the artwork publicly, please credit Beewaker and do not remove the artist's signature.

Resale and any other commercial use are prohibited unless agreed in advance.
