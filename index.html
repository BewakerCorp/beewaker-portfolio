How to order

I mostly work through auctions on YCH Commishes. You can check current slots and join there.

If you want something more specific or just want to discuss an idea — feel free to message me on Discord (beewaker). Just send references and a short description of what you have in mind. I’ll take a look and let you know if I can take it and under what conditions.

Payment

Once we agree on the details and I confirm the order, payment is made through LavaTop.

Please don’t pay before I accept the order.

Turnaround

From two weeks after receiving all materials and payment (can be earlier).

Exact timing is discussed individually.

Usage

The finished artwork can be used for personal purposes (avatar, wallpaper, posts, etc.) and reposted. If you mention me next to the commission, I’d really appreciate it.

I reserve the right to use the finished artwork in my portfolio and social media. If you are against this, it must be discussed in advance.

Commercial use (resale, merch, advertising, etc.) is only allowed by separate agreement.
